# Executive Writing Dashboard Report

## Dashboard Overview
The Executive Writing Dashboard provides School Principals and Supervisors with a real-time, quantitative view of document quality across the Tampuyak OS platform.

## Key Indicators Displayed
- **Overall Writing Quality Score:** Aggregate score of narrative richness and logic.
- **Narrative Diversity Index:** Ensures paragraphs aren't being overly repeated across different sections.
- **Regulatory Coverage:** Percentage of strategic priorities explicitly backed by a cited Permendikbudristek.
- **Terminology Adherence:** Real-time check against the Master Dictionary.
- **Print Readiness Certification:** A binary PASS/FAIL indicating if the document is legally and structurally ready for physical distribution.

## Purpose
The dashboard transitions the evaluation of document quality from a subjective human review to an objective, automated metric, accelerating the approval process.
