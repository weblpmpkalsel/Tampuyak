# KSP GOLD M4.0: BAB IV ENTERPRISE AUDIT

## 1. GOVERNMENT STRUCTURE (KSP/KOSP REGULATIONS)
According to the latest Indonesian Ministry of Education (Kemendikbudristek) guidelines for Kurikulum Satuan Pendidikan (KSP), BAB IV is titled **Pendampingan, Evaluasi, dan Pengembangan Profesional/Kurikulum**.

The mandatory sub-sections include:
* **A. Pendampingan Implementasi Kurikulum:** Mentoring, coaching, and supervision strategies for teachers. (Mandatory)
* **B. Evaluasi:** Covering Monitoring, Evaluasi, Refleksi, and Tindak Lanjut of the curriculum implementation. (Mandatory)
* **C. Pengembangan Profesional / Kurikulum:** Capacity building plans for educators and educational staff, as well as curriculum refinement strategies. (Mandatory)

## 2. KNOWLEDGE CLASSIFICATION
Every subsection maps to specific knowledge domains within the enterprise architecture:
* **Pendampingan:** Operational Knowledge / Planning Knowledge (execution strategies).
* **Monitoring & Evaluasi:** Strategic Knowledge (metrics, KPIs, baseline data).
* **Refleksi & Tindak Lanjut:** Operational Knowledge (feedback loops).
* **Pengembangan Profesional/Kurikulum:** Planning Knowledge & School Knowledge (HR capacity building).

## 3. SOURCE OF TRUTH
To ensure zero duplication, the authoritative sources are strictly defined:
* **Pendampingan & Pengembangan:** `PlanningIntelligenceEngine` and `RKT` (Rencana Kerja Tahunan) containing scheduled capacity-building activities.
* **Evaluasi & Refleksi:** `RaporIntelligenceEngine` and `EvaluationService` providing baseline metrics and strategic evaluation methods.
* **Personnel Targets:** `MasterData` (Guru/Tendik) for identifying subjects of professional development.

## 4. OWNERSHIP
* **Kepala Sekolah (Principal):** Ultimate owner of the evaluation and monitoring framework.
* **Tim Pengembang Kurikulum (TPK):** Owners of the curriculum development and professional mentoring schedules.
* **Pengawas Sekolah (School Supervisor):** External validator and evaluator.

## 5. BUILDER STRATEGY
Builders for BAB IV must remain **pure consumers**. They will query:
1. `EvaluationService` for assessment frameworks.
2. `PlanningService` / `RKT` for mentoring and training schedules.
3. `SchoolContextService` for existing HR capacity.
The Builder will not hold state or business logic; it merely transforms domain knowledge into the Canonical payload for BAB IV.

## 6. ASSEMBLER STRATEGY
The `KspDocumentAssembler` will ingest the `Bab4Payload` consisting of `EvaluationKnowledge` and `PlanningKnowledge`. 
It will orchestrate the sequential generation of Section A (Pendampingan), Section B (Evaluasi), and Section C (Pengembangan Profesional) strictly isolating this from previous chapters.

## 7. CANONICAL MAPPING
The existing Canonical Document schema fully supports BAB IV requirements without modification:
* **Framework Descriptions:** `ParagraphBlock`, `ListBlock`.
* **Schedules & Matrices (Mentoring/Training):** `TableBlock`.
* **Structure:** `HeadingBlock` (H1 for Chapter, H2 for sub-sections).
No new block types or extensions are required.

## 8. REGULATORY FIDELITY
The proposed architecture natively maps to Kemendikbudristek requirements, ensuring the KSP generated contains actionable, measurable, and integrated evaluation and professional development plans tied directly to RKT and Rapor Pendidikan.

## 9. RISK ASSESSMENT
* **Architecture Risk:** LOW. Canonical types already support required layouts.
* **Regression Risk:** ZERO. BAB IV is generated as a standalone section appended by the Assembler. It does not mutate or depend on BAB I, II, or III states.
* **Data Availability Risk:** MEDIUM. Requires ensuring that `PlanningEngine` (RKT activities) contains valid professional development entries to populate tables adequately. Mitigated by fallback default texts if data is sparse.

==================================================
## FINAL DECISION
**GO**

BAB IV architecture is sound, perfectly aligns with enterprise constraints, respects source-of-truth mandates, and can be implemented via the standard Builder-Assembler pattern without risk of regression.
