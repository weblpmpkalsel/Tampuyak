# NATIONAL EDUCATION KNOWLEDGE BASE REPORT

## Overview
A new centralized, policy-driven architecture called the National Education Knowledge Base (NEKB) has been successfully established at `src/NEKB.ts`. This replaces all static narratives and fragmented registries.

## Knowledge Domains Implemented
- **Module A: Government Structure**: Standardizes ministry identity and educational governance.
- **Module B: National Education Policies**: Defines current educational philosophies and national priorities.
- **Module C: Graduate Profile**: Strictly enforces the official 8 Dimensi Profil Lulusan.
- **Module D: Curriculum Development**: Standardizes KSP, Learning Organization, and Planning terminology.
- **Module E: Planning Documents**: Defines structural standards for RKJM, RKT, RKAS, Monitoring, and Evaluation.
- **Module F: Educational Quality**: Sets standards around Rapor Pendidikan, School DNA, and continuous improvement cycles.

## Compliance
All core modules including `KSPIntelligenceEngine` and `Government Writing Engine` (GWE) now retrieve terminologies directly from this single source of truth. No educational concept is invented or sourced from legacy variables.
