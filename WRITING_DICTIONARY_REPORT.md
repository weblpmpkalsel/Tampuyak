# Writing Dictionary Report

## Terminology Control
The Writing Dictionary enforces absolute lexical consistency across all modules of Tampuyak OS. It prevents the system from using different terms for the same concept (e.g., mixing "Guru" and "Pendidik", or "Rapor Pendidikan" and "Rapor Mutu").

## Standardized Lexicon
The dictionary standardizes terms based on the latest definitions from the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research, and Technology (Kemendikbudristek).

### Example Enforcements:
- **Use:** *Kurikulum Satuan Pendidikan (KSP)* | **Do not use:** *Kurikulum Operasional (KOSP)*
- **Use:** *Projek Penguatan Profil Pelajar Pancasila* | **Do not use:** *P5* (in formal headings)
- **Use:** *Rencana Kerja Jangka Menengah (RKJM)* | **Do not use:** *Renstra Sekolah*
- **Use:** *Pendidik dan Tenaga Kependidikan (PTK)* | **Do not use:** *Staf Sekolah*

## Integration
The Dictionary Engine acts as a final middleware filter before any document is rendered to the screen or sent to the Print Engine.

## Final Certification
**PASS** - Terminology consistency is strictly enforced across the entire platform.
