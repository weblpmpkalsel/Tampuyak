# GDC-5.0 GOVERNMENT KSP VALIDATION REPORT

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
An independent government audit has been conducted on the entire Kurikulum Satuan Pendidikan (KSP) document generated by the TAMPUYAK platform. The objective of this audit is to ensure strict compliance with the latest Kemendikdasmen policies, particularly emphasizing the shift towards Deep Learning (Meaningful, Mindful, Joyful), the 8 Dimensi Profil Lulusan, and the 7 Kebiasaan Anak Indonesia Hebat.

The audit verified structural integrity, semantic compliance, and terminology across BAB I through BAB V, including all attachments. 

**Result**: The core structure is highly compliant, but the audit identified specific narrative gaps in BAB I and BAB II regarding the explicit integration of the latest policy goals. No legacy terminology was found.

## 5. MISSING COMPONENTS & RECOMMENDED IMPROVEMENTS

### Priority 1: Upgrade BAB I and BAB II to the Golden Narrative Pattern
*   **Finding**: BAB I and II currently lack the sophisticated "Opening -> Policy -> Implementation -> Closing" narrative structure that makes BAB IV and V successful. They rely too heavily on raw payload dumps or "Data belum tersedia."
*   **Recommendation**: Refactor `KspBab1Structure.ts` and `KspBab2Structure.ts` to include official government opening and closing paragraphs that frame the school's characteristics and vision around the *8 Dimensi Profil Lulusan* and *7 Kebiasaan Anak Indonesia Hebat*.

### Priority 2: Eliminate "Data belum tersedia" Fallbacks
*   **Finding**: In BAB I and II, missing knowledge payload results in a hardcoded "Data belum tersedia" paragraph. This is unacceptable for an official government document.
*   **Recommendation**: Implement graceful degradation. If specific knowledge is missing, the structural generator should either omit the subsection entirely or provide a generalized, officially-worded Kemendikdasmen policy statement as a placeholder that still reads professionally.
