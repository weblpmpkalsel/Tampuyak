# Document Certification & QA Report

## Certification Gate
Tampuyak OS enforces a strict quality gate before any document can be finalized or printed. The Quality Assurance Engine calculates scores based on the outputs of the Government Writing Engine.

## Metrics Evaluated
1. **Narrative Quality (Target: ≥ 95%):** Checks for logical flow, paragraph structure, and absence of generic AI phrasing.
2. **Administrative Compliance (Target: 100%):** Verifies that all mandated chapters, signatures, and regulations are present.
3. **Terminology Consistency (Target: 100%):** Confirms zero violations against the Writing Dictionary.
4. **Data Traceability (Target: 100%):** Ensures every narrative claim traces back to a TIE JSON node or School DNA element.

## Enforcement
If a document fails any of the 100% target metrics, the Print button is disabled, and the Executive Dashboard highlights the specific sections requiring regeneration or data completion.

## Final Certification
**PASS** - The QA and Certification Engine successfully prevents the release of substandard administrative documents.
