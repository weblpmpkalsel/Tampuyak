# W3.1 ENTERPRISE DOCUMENT LIFECYCLE (EDLC)

## 1. ENTERPRISE PRINCIPLE
A document is NOT a file. A document is an Enterprise Knowledge Asset. PDF is only one publication format, and PDF SHALL NOT become the source of truth. The underlying knowledge graph and metadata represent the actual asset.

## 2. LIFECYCLE DIAGRAM
```text
[ Source Selection ] -> (Template, Upload, Copied, AI Draft)
         |
         v
  (1) Knowledge Created
         |
         v
  (2) Draft (Editing Phase)
         |
         v
  (3) In Review
         |
         v
  (4) Approved
         |
         v
  (5) Published (PDF generation, Print, Archive)
         |
         v
  (6) Referenced (Consumed by KSP, RKJM, RKAS, SPMI)
         |
         v
  (7) Version Updated (Loops back to Draft/Review)
         |
         v
  (8) Archived (End of Lifecycle, Replaced by new academic year/version)
```

## 3. STATE MACHINE
- **Knowledge Created:** Instantiated from an Official Template, School Template, Community Template, AI Draft, Uploaded Document, or Copied from a Previous Year.
- **Draft:** Work-in-progress. Mutable by authorized owners (School, Teacher, Principal, Curriculum Team, Operator).
- **In Review:** Submitted for evaluation by an authorized role.
- **Approved:** Locked for editing. Ready for publication.
- **Published:** Official representation available for downstream systems, reporting, and PDF generation.
- **Referenced:** Actively linked by Enterprise Modules.
- **Archived:** Historical record. Immutable and no longer active for the current academic cycle.

## 4. VERSION STRATEGY
Every revision creates a traceable footprint:
- **Version Number:** Incremental identifier (e.g., v1, v2).
- **Revision Date & Author:** Track who made changes and when.
- **Revision Note:** Description of what changed in this version.
- **Current Active Version:** The canonical version currently approved for use.
- **Rollback Capability:** Ability to safely revert to any previous version.

## 5. PUBLICATION STRATEGY
PDF is merely an output artifact, not the knowledge itself.
`Knowledge -> Document -> Preview -> PDF -> Print -> Digital Archive`
The structured data and metadata remain the ultimate source of truth, enabling dynamic recomposition, AI analysis, and system-wide querying.

## 6. REFERENCE STRATEGY
One document -> Many consumers.
Documents can be referenced simultaneously by KSP, RKJM, RKT, RKAS, SPMI, Supervision, Accreditation, Adiwiyata, and Future Enterprise Modules.
No duplication allowed. References strictly use unique Document IDs, maintaining a single source of truth across the entire enterprise.

## 7. AUDIT TRAIL
Every document SHALL maintain an immutable audit history:
- Created By
- Created Date
- Modified By
- Modified Date
- Approval Date
- Published Date
- Archive Date

## 8. GOVERNMENT FIDELITY STRATEGY
- **Immutable Templates:** Official government templates remain read-only to ensure absolute fidelity to national standards.
- **Customization:** Schools may clone and customize official templates for their local context.
- **Preservation:** The system preserves the original template baseline alongside the customized school version and its full approval history, ensuring compliance audits can trace derivations accurately.

## 9. FUTURE WORKFLOW STRATEGY
Reserved architecture support for advanced governance features (No implementation yet):
- Digital Signature & QR Verification
- Electronic & Workflow Approval
- Multi-stage Review
- **AI Assistance:** Draft Generation, Compliance Suggestions, Revision Suggestions, Difference Comparison, Policy Recommendation.

## 10. ADR-011: ENTERPRISE DOCUMENT LIFECYCLE
**Context:** The platform needs a robust, formalized process for document evolution to ensure knowledge assets are treated as living, compliant, and reusable enterprise entities, rather than static files.
**Decision:** Implement the Enterprise Document Lifecycle (EDLC) state machine to govern the creation, review, approval, publication, referencing, and archival of all documents. Establish that structured knowledge is the source of truth, not PDF outputs.
**Consequences:**
- Guarantees regulatory compliance through strict audit trails and versioning.
- Prevents duplication by enforcing centralized referencing.
- Enables future advanced workflows like digital signatures and AI-assisted governance without architectural rewrites.

## FINAL DECISION
**GO**

The Enterprise Document Lifecycle architecture successfully establishes a scalable, secure, and intelligent governance model for all knowledge assets across the TAMPUYAK Enterprise Platform.
