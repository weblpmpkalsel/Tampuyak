# SECTION REVIEW REPORT

## 1. Overview
Tracks the review status on a per-chapter/per-section basis across all documents.

## 2. Section Review Protocol
1. Open the document.
2. Click the QA Mode icon in the toolbar.
3. Select a section from the left navigation pane.
4. The QA Panel on the right will update to reflect the `Active Section`.
5. Read the narrative, review the tables, and assign the appropriate Status, Severity, and Comments.
