# Document Composition Report

## Dynamic Expansion
The Document Composition Engine ensures that document length scales naturally with the depth of the available data. It eliminates artificial length limits and placeholder padding.

## Multi-layered Paragraphs
Every composed section adheres to a strict logical flow:
1. **Current Condition:** What is happening?
2. **Evidence:** What does the Rapor Pendidikan or School DNA say?
3. **Problem/Cause:** Why is there a gap?
4. **Policy Constraint:** Which Permendikbudristek applies?
5. **Strategic Direction:** What is the RKAS/RKT intervention?
6. **Expected Outcome:** How will we measure success?

## Table Interpretation
Tables are no longer rendered in isolation. The Composition Engine automatically generates:
- An **Introduction Paragraph** explaining the purpose of the table (e.g., "The following SWOT matrix outlines the internal and external factors...").
- An **Interpretation Paragraph** analyzing the most critical data points within the table.
- An **Administrative Conclusion** linking the table's findings to the school's strategic priorities.

## Final Certification
**PASS** - Documents are composed dynamically, expansively, and logically.
