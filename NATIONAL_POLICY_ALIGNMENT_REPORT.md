# NATIONAL POLICY ALIGNMENT REPORT

## Overview
Tampuyak OS has undergone a complete master policy review to align with the latest national education guidelines from Kemendikdasmen. 

## Key Updates
- **Ministry Identity**: All legacy references to "Kemendikbudristek" and "Kemendikbud" have been systematically audited and replaced with "Kemendikdasmen".
- **Regulatory Framework**: The active regulations have been updated to reflect current governance structures.
- **Master Policy Registry**: A centralized registry `MasterPolicyRegistry.ts` has been instituted as the single source of truth for educational terminology.
