# Enterprise KSP Dynamic Engine Report

## KSP as a Living Document
The Kurikulum Satuan Pendidikan (KSP) in Tampuyak OS is no longer a static, manually typed document. It is a dynamic composition engine that aggregates data from the School DNA Engine and Master Education Platform.

## Chapter Synchronization
- **BAB I (Karakteristik Sekolah):** Dynamically generated from the School DNA Engine's contextual and cultural classifications.
- **BAB II (Visi, Misi, Tujuan):** Synchronized directly from the School DNA identity matrix. Edits to the DNA automatically update this chapter.
- **BAB III (Pengorganisasian Pembelajaran):** Structured around Intracurricular, Co-curricular (P5), and Extracurricular data.
- **BAB IV (Perencanaan Pembelajaran):** Pulls from Teacher Characteristics and Digital Readiness metrics.
- **BAB V (Pendampingan & Evaluasi):** Synchronizes with the RKT and RKAS monitoring frameworks.

## Elimination of Duplication
Users no longer type the Vision and Mission into the KSP editor. The editor locks these fields and displays a read-only view mapped to the DNA Engine. If a user wishes to change the Vision, they must change the School DNA.

## Final Certification
**PASS** - The KSP engine operates as a fully dynamic composition layer, continuously synchronized with the school's evolving identity and planning decisions.
