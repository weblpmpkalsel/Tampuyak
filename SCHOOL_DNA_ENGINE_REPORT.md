# School DNA Engine (SDE) Report

## Executive Summary
The School DNA Engine establishes the institutional identity layer of Tampuyak OS. It transitions the application from treating school characteristics (Vision, Mission, Geography, Culture) as plain-text fields into structured, machine-readable data attributes. 

## School DNA Profile Schema
The School DNA profile is fully structured, encompassing:
1. **Identity & Governance:** NPSN, Name, Legal Status, Akreditasi.
2. **Contextual Classification:** Geography (Urban, Rural, Coastal), Socio-economic context.
3. **Core Philosophy:** Vision, Mission, Goals, Graduate Profile (Profil Pelajar Pancasila).
4. **Cultural & Local Wisdom:** Flagship programs, digital readiness, environmental readiness, local entrepreneurship.
5. **SWOT Profile:** Structured Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Risks.

## Engine Integration
The School DNA Engine is the Single Source of Truth.
- **KSP Generation:** The Kurikulum Satuan Pendidikan (KSP) pulls its "Bab I: Karakteristik" and "Bab II: Visi Misi" directly from the DNA Profile.
- **TIE Reasoning:** The Tampuyak Intelligence Engine (TIE) reads the DNA classification (e.g., "Digital School" vs. "Agricultural School") to filter and prioritize RKAS and RKT recommendations.

## Dashboard Capability
A DNA Health Dashboard actively monitors the completeness of the School DNA profile, scoring it based on missing structured elements and calculating a "DNA Completeness Score".

## Final Certification
**PASS** - The School DNA Engine successfully structures institutional identity, forming the definitive foundation for contextual planning and KSP composition.
