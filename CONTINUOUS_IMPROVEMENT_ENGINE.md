# CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT ENGINE
## TAMPUYAK OS ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE

### 1. OVERVIEW
The Continuous Improvement Cycle defines the macroeconomic operational heartbeat of the school. It integrates the Planning Engine and the Quality Engine into a closed-loop feedback system, ensuring that strategic goals are executed, measured, and iteratively improved upon.

### 2. THE COMPLETE CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT LIFECYCLE

#### Phase 1: Strategic Direction
1. **School Vision:** The ultimate north star of the institution.
2. **KSP (Kurikulum Satuan Pendidikan):** The pedagogical translation of the vision into curriculum organization and school culture.

#### Phase 2: Strategic & Tactical Planning (Planning Engine)
3. **RKJM (Rencana Kerja Jangka Menengah):** 4-year strategic roadmap defining macro-objectives.
4. **RKT (Rencana Kerja Tahunan):** 1-year tactical plan extracting actionable programs from the RKJM.
5. **RKAS (Rencana Kegiatan dan Anggaran Sekolah):** The financial translation of the RKT into funded, scheduled activities.
6. **Implementation:** The execution of programs across the Academic Calendar.

#### Phase 3: Evaluation & Measurement (Quality Engine)
7. **Academic Supervision:** Systematic observation of the implementation (specifically teaching and learning processes).
8. **Evidence:** Collection of artifacts (modules, logs, media) validating the quality and occurrence of the implementation.
9. **RTL (Rencana Tindak Lanjut):** Immediate corrective actions at the micro-level (e.g., individual teacher coaching, specific pedagogical training).

#### Phase 4: Synthesis & Feedback
10. **Monitoring:** Administrative tracking of RTL completion and short-term outcomes.
11. **Quality Analytics:** Aggregation of supervision scores, RTL completion rates, and evidence quality across the entire school.
12. **Recommendation:** Data-driven proposals for systemic improvement generated by the Quality Engine.

#### Phase 5: Recalibration
13. **Planning Review:** School Executives evaluate the Recommendations against the current RKT and RKJM.
14. **Next RKJM / RKT:** Strategic adjustments are codified into the subsequent planning cycles, closing the loop.

### 3. THE CYCLE'S INTEGRITY
- **Unidirectional Flow of Authority:** Planning dictates Execution. Execution dictates Measurement. Measurement proposes adjustments. 
- **The Human-in-the-Loop Constraint:** The transition from Phase 4 (Recommendation) back to Phase 2 (Planning) ALWAYS requires Executive approval. The system informs, but humans decide.
