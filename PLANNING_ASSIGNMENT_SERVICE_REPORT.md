# RKJM ENGINE PHASE 2.5.3 - PLANNING ASSIGNMENT SERVICE

## 1. Architecture Review
The `PlanningAssignmentService` has been introduced as an isolated decision layer. It sits between the strategic domain (`AnnualPlanningActivity`) and the operational scheduling domain (`PlanningDistribution`). It consumes the existing `PlanningDistribution` rules, AI recommendations, and context to produce a deterministic `PlanningAssignment`.

## 2. Assignment Domain Diagram
```text
[AnnualPlanningActivity] (Strategic Source)
       ↓
[PlanningAssignmentService] (Decision Maker)
       ↓ (Reads PlanningDistribution, Recommendations, Rules)
[PlanningAssignment] (Decision Record)
       ↓
[OperationalPlanningActivity] (Execution Carrier)
```

## 3. PlanningAssignment Model
`PlanningAssignment` records the definitive decision of an activity's scheduling:
- **Identifier & Reference**: `assignmentId`, `activityReferenceId`
- **Context**: `planningCycleId`, `planningSnapshotVersion`, `businessRuleVersion`, `schemaVersion`, `assignmentVersion`
- **Decision Details**: `planningYear`, `semester`, `quarter`, `priority`, `sequence`
- **Dependencies**: `dependencyReferences`, `recommendationReferences`
- **Provenance**: `assignmentSource`, `assignmentReason`, `assignmentStatus`, `createdAt`, `createdBy`, `approvedAt`, `approvedBy`

## 4. Assignment Lifecycle
1. **DRAFT**: Initial decision generated by the engine or manual save.
2. **SUGGESTED**: A decision originating purely from `FUTURE_AI_RECOMMENDATION`.
3. **REVIEWED**: Acknowledged by a reviewer but not yet finalized.
4. **APPROVED**: Decision finalized; the activity is officially slated for that Planning Year.
5. **REJECTED**: The assignment was declined (likely necessitating a reassignment).
6. **ARCHIVED**: Kept for historical traceability after cycle completion.

## 5. Assignment Responsibility Matrix
- **PlanningAssignmentService**: Only makes decisions. Returns `PlanningAssignment`.
- **PlanningAssignment**: Represents a discrete decision. It does NOT mutate execution or push data.
- **AnnualPlans Generator (Future Phase)**: Consumes the `PlanningAssignment` decision to physically distribute activities into `Y1`, `Y2`, `Y3`, `Y4`.

## 6. Reference Strategy
The `PlanningAssignment` relies solely on `activityReferenceId`. It strictly prevents duplication by avoiding copying the program, target, or budget descriptions. It references the upstream `AnnualPlanningActivity`.

## 7. Future Distribution Integration
In the next phase, the existing distribution logic (`annualPlans` generator in `DocumentSynchronizationEngine` and `Tampuyak.tsx`) will be refactored to consume `PlanningAssignmentService`. Instead of blindly calculating a round-robin year, it will request an assignment decision, verify the `assignmentStatus`, and subsequently distribute the activity into the designated `annualPlans[targetYear]` bucket.

## 8. Backward Compatibility Analysis
- **Zero Impact on Execution**: The service does not write any state or manipulate any global variables.
- **Zero Impact on Strategic Domain**: `AnnualPlanningActivity` was deliberately left untouched.
- **Zero Impact on Generators**: `annualPlans` pipeline and `Chapter4Builder` remain exactly as they were in Phase 2.5.1.
- **Isolated Addition**: The new models and service live completely isolated inside `/src/planning/`.

## 9. Risk Analysis
- **Architectural Risk**: Extremely low. The architecture correctly separates decision (Assignment) from execution (Distribution).
- **Domain Corruption**: Zero, as all references are ID-based rather than copy-based.
- **Implementation Risk**: Zero, as the new service is completely unhooked from the active pipelines right now, serving as a clean target for future phases.
