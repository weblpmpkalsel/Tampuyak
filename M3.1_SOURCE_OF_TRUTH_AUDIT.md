## 2. Special Analysis & Duplication Audit

### Curriculum Structure & Learning Load
- **Origin**: National standards define the baseline. The school defines the exact allocation.
- **Duplication Risk**: High. If KSP hardcodes these tables, they will fall out of sync with actual teacher schedules.
- **Resolution**: KSP MUST consume these purely from the `LearningOrganizationKnowledge` payload. The data belongs in a centralized Master Curriculum Registry. KSP is a read-only consumer of the final computed load.

### Intracurricular & Co-curricular (P5)
- **Origin**: The narrative policy and structural approach (e.g., Block vs. Subject scheduling) belong to School Operational Knowledge.
- **Resolution**: KSP renders the high-level policy. Detailed execution (specific themes, specific subjects) must map directly from the active curriculum metadata.

### Teaching Modules & ATP
- **Origin**: Created and managed by teachers.
- **Duplication Risk**: Critical. Embedding them in KSP creates immediate staleness and bloat.
- **Resolution**: MUST remain in the Teacher Workspace. KSP will ONLY reference them via the `ATTACHMENT` Canonical Block (Attachment Gateway).

### Academic Calendar
- **Origin**: Regional Education Office sets the baseline; the school maps actual activities.
- **Resolution**: Should not be manually typed into KSP. Must be derived from a global Academic Calendar Module/Registry.

## 3. Enterprise Boundary Audit
- **Single Source of Truth**: Preserved. Volatile data (ATP, Moduls) is strictly isolated to Teacher domains.
- **Builder Purity**: Preserved. `LearningOrganizationBuilder` and `CurriculumStructureBuilder` will only parse and normalize incoming metadata.
- **Assembler Responsibility**: Preserved. `KspDocumentAssembler` will orchestrate the payloads without containing business logic.
- **Government Fidelity**: Preserved. Static regulatory bounds are respected; no AI paraphrasing of core laws.
