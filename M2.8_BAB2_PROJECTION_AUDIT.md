# M2.8 BAB II PROJECTION AUDIT

## 1. Projection Change Gate
This sprint is strictly limited to modifying the BAB II projection layer. It is authorized to alter how the Enterprise Strategic Knowledge (Vision, Mission, and Goals) is mapped into Canonical Document Blocks within `KspBab2Structure.ts`. It may also update the `KspDocumentAssembler.ts` to properly pass the Strategic Knowledge Payload to the BAB II builder. Nothing else is allowed to change.

## 2. Boundary Audit
The following components are strictly **LOCKED** and verified to remain untouched during this sprint:
- Universal Renderer
- Publishing Engine
- Canonical Domain Schema (`CanonicalDocumentTypes.ts`)
- Workspace components
- Metadata Adapter (`KspMetadataAdapter.ts`)
- School Knowledge Service/Builder
- Regulatory Knowledge Service/Builder
- BAB I (`KspBab1Structure.ts`)
- BAB III–BAB V (`KspBab3Structure.ts`, `KspBab4Structure.ts`, `KspBab5Structure.ts`)

## 3. Projection Mapping Audit
The projection of each Enterprise Knowledge Domain into BAB II follows strict structural rules:
- **Vision Knowledge** ↓ Heading (Level 2) ↓ Paragraph
- **Mission Knowledge** ↓ Heading (Level 2) ↓ Ordered List
- **Goals Knowledge** ↓ Heading (Level 2) ↓ Ordered List

*Government Fidelity:* This structural projection perfectly satisfies Government Fidelity. Official KSP guidelines require clear, unmodified presentation of the school's Vision, Mission, and Goals. Using structural headings and ordered lists ensures they are presented identically to the school's official decrees without narrative alteration.

## 4. Canonical Projection Audit
The projection uses **ONLY** existing Canonical Blocks.
- **Allowed used:** `HEADING`, `PARAGRAPH`, `LIST`, `LIST_ITEM`, `TEXT`
- **Forbidden:** No new block types are introduced. No presentation blocks or custom layout blocks are utilized. The structure relies entirely on the established semantic Canonical Domain.

## 5. Government Fidelity Audit
BAB II rigorously follows the official KSP document structure:
- **Title hierarchy**: Chapter title ("VISI, MISI, DAN TUJUAN") is Level 1.
- **Heading levels**: Sections (Visi, Misi, Tujuan) are Level 2.
- **Mission numbering**: Enforced via `ordered: true` on the `LIST` block.
- **Goal numbering**: Enforced via `ordered: true` on the `LIST` block.
- **Administrative consistency**: The projection accurately maintains the exact wording provided by the Knowledge layer.

## 6. TOC Projection Audit
The projected headings (VISI, MISI, TUJUAN) automatically appear inside the Table of Contents because they use the standard Canonical `HEADING` blocks. The Universal Renderer's TOC generator dynamically reads the Canonical AST and extracts blocks of type `HEADING`. No hardcoding is required.

## 7. Builder Projection Audit
**Builder Responsibilities Verified:**
- **Builder SHALL**: Receive Knowledge payload ↓ Project Knowledge structurally ↓ Generate Canonical Blocks.
- **Builder SHALL NOT**: Rewrite Vision, Mission, or Goals. It does not improve wording, interpret knowledge, summarize, or calculate. It acts purely as a passthrough to the canonical AST.

## 8. Assembler Projection Audit
**Assembler Responsibilities Verified:**
- **Assembler SHALL**: Compose Vision, Mission, and Goals from the Knowledge Service/Builder ↓ Send them precisely to the BAB II Builder.
- **Assembler SHALL NOT**: Render, format, or generate narrative text. It solely coordinates the flow of the Enterprise Knowledge Object to the Builder.

## 9. File Impact Audit
Files required for BAB II projection:

  - *Purpose*: Maps the Vision, Mission, and Goals knowledge into Canonical Blocks.
  - *Risk*: LOW

- `src/enterprise/document/ksp/KspDocumentAssembler.ts`
  - *Purpose*: Orchestrates the injection of the Strategic Knowledge into the BAB II builder.

Everything else remains **LOCKED**.

## 10. Regression Audit
Projection changes in BAB II cannot affect:
- **BAB I, Front Matter, BAB III-V**: They are structurally independent builder modules invoked separately by the Assembler.
- **Renderer / Publishing**: They consume the final Canonical AST regardless of the source.
- **Planning / RKJM / RKAS**: These consume the Enterprise Knowledge directly, entirely bypassing the document projection layer.

## 11. Risk Assessment
- **Architecture Risk**: **LOW**. Reinforces the separation of knowledge and projection.
- **Regression Risk**: **LOW**. Componentized document sections ensure changes in BAB II do not leak.
- **Projection Risk**: **LOW**. Utilizes battle-tested Canonical Block mappings (Paragraphs, Lists).
- **Government Fidelity Risk**: **LOW**. Direct mapping without AI interpretation guarantees 100% fidelity.
- **Maintenance Risk**: **LOW**. The mapping is straightforward and highly readable.
*Mitigation*: Strict adherence to the Builder and Assembler rules ensures these risks remain nominal.

## 12. Implementation Readiness
**Recommendation**: **GO**
The BAB II projection is architecturally sound, safe from regression, adheres strictly to Government Fidelity, and relies entirely on existing, stable Canonical Blocks. It is fully ready for implementation.
