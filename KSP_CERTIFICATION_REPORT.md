# KSP CERTIFICATION REPORT

## Objective
To algorithmically verify that the generated KSP meets all national standards before allowing export or finalization.

## Certification Checks
The `certifyKSP()` method in the Intelligence Engine evaluates:
1. **Official Component Completeness**: Are all 5 mandatory components present?
2. **Educational Consistency**: Does the Mission actually support the Vision?
3. **Evidence Mapping**: Is the characteristic analysis backed by actual Rapor Pendidikan data?
4. **Regulation Compliance**: Does it adhere to the Panduan KSP Revisi 2025?
5. **Graduate Profile Alignment**: Are the 8 Dimensions integrated into the Learning Organization?

## Status
PASS. The Certification Engine successfully validates the KSP against the new 2025 structural and logical requirements.
