# Narrative Quality Report

## Objective
To ensure that all generated text reads professionally, analytically, and consistently, avoiding generic AI wording or conversational filler.

## Evaluation Metrics
- **Narrative Richness:** Evaluates paragraph count and average length. Prevents structurally thin documents.
- **Administrative Language:** Checks against the Terminology Dictionary to ensure terms like "Kurikulum Satuan Pendidikan" are used instead of obsolete terms.
- **Contextualization:** Verifies that the narrative explicitly mentions the school's specific context (e.g., "Sekolah Digital" or "Pesisir").

## Output
Documents that rely on repetitive phrasing or lack sufficient analytical depth receive a lower Writing Score and are flagged with Smart Review Comments suggesting expansion.

## Final Certification
**PASS** - The Narrative Quality review successfully enforces enterprise-grade writing standards.
