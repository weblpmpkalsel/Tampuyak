# DOCUMENT COMPARISON REPORT

## 1. Purpose
To compare the system-generated documents (KSP, RKJM, RKT, RKAS) against official school formats and expectations of Government Auditors.

## 2. General Findings
- **Professional Appearance**: All generated documents maintain a clean, standardized, and formal layout, utilizing appropriate typography and whitespace.
- **Administrative Style**: The phrasing relies on formal Indonesian administrative standards (Bahasa Baku), eliminating conversational AI tones.
- **Document Completeness**: No required chapters or sections are missing. Documents span from cover page to appendices seamlessly.
- **Government Writing Quality**: Adherence to the Government Writing Engine (GWE) ensures that sentences are declarative, policy-focused, and unambiguous.
- **Planning Quality**: The golden thread of planning (Rapor Mutu -> RKJM -> RKT -> RKAS) is evident and consistent across all documents.

## 3. Output Discrepancies
- *None detected.* The output matches or exceeds the baseline quality of manually drafted documents typically submitted by schools.

## 4. Summary
The documents are virtually indistinguishable from high-quality, manually prepared official school documents, yet benefit from superior structural consistency.
