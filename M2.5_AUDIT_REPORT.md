## 3. Goals Knowledge Audit
- **Purpose**: Defines specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, and time-bound (SMART) educational and institutional outcomes. Goals translate the strategic Mission into observable achievements.
- **Lifecycle**: Short-to-medium term. Typically defined and reviewed on an annual or bi-annual basis, aligning with academic years and Rencana Kerja Tahunan (RKT).
- **Ownership**: The School's Governing Body, Principal, and Tim Pengembang Kurikulum (TPK).
- **Consumers**: KSP Document Builders (BAB II), RKT/RKAS Planning Engines, Evaluation & Assessment Engines, and Accreditation Authorities.

## 4. Knowledge Ownership Audit
- **School**: The true authoritative owner. The school determines its specific, contextual targets.
- **Government**: Provides the overarching national standards (e.g., Standar Nasional Pendidikan, Rapor Pendidikan), but does not own the specific institutional goals.
- **Workspace**: Serves only as a temporary conduit.
- **Future Registry**: Will act as the enterprise system of record and technical owner of the digital asset.

## 5. Knowledge Source Audit
- **Origin**: Goals originate from the school's internal deliberation, often informed by Rapor Pendidikan (Education Report Card) evaluations.
- **Current Flow**: Manually entered by the user and channeled through Workspace Metadata into the monolithic `VisionMissionPayload`.
- **Future Flow**: Will be deterministically queried from the Future Registry. The system must never auto-generate or infer goals.

## 6. Knowledge Independence Audit
- **From Vision**: Vision is the aspirational destination. Goals are the specific milestones. A Vision persists, but Goals change when achieved or recalibrated.
- **From Mission**: Mission is the strategic action plan. Goals are the measurable targets of those actions.
- **From School Knowledge**: A school's physical infrastructure or demographics may contextualize goals, but they are separate domains.
- **From Regulatory Knowledge**: Regulations dictate legal standards; Goals dictate institutional ambitions.

## 7. Relationship Audit
**Strategic Chain**:
`School Knowledge` (Current Reality & Resources)
  ↓ contextualizes
`Vision Knowledge` (Ultimate Aspirational Destination)
  ↓ drives
`Mission Knowledge` (Strategic Commitments & Actions)
  ↓ quantifies into
`Goals Knowledge` (Measurable Short-term Milestones)
  ↓ breaks down into
`Program Knowledge` (Specific initiatives in RKJM/RKT)
  ↓ executed via
`Activity Knowledge` (Daily/Weekly actions and RKAS budgeting)
  ↓
`Document Projection` (The Document Builder simply projects these independent domains into readable formats, like BAB II).

## 8. Measurability Audit
- **Goals are measurable**: They specify targets (e.g., "Meningkatkan rata-rata nilai numerasi sebesar 10%").
- **Goals are not strategies**: Strategies belong in the Mission or Programs.
- **Goals are not activities**: Activities belong in operational planning (RKT/RKAS).
- **Goals are not Vision**: They are finite and achievable, whereas Vision is continuous and aspirational.

## 9. Builder Audit
Builders must remain pure Consumers. A Builder will map the `GoalsKnowledgePayload` strictly into a `LIST` or `PARAGRAPH` Canonical Block.
Builders shall never:
- Rewrite, summarize, or generate goals.
- Improve goals using AI to make them "more measurable."
- Interpret whether a goal is achieved.

## 10. Assembler Audit
The `KspDocumentAssembler` operates as the orchestrator:
1. Fetch `SchoolKnowledgePayload`.
2. Fetch `RegulatoryKnowledgePayload`.
3. Fetch `VisionKnowledgePayload`.
4. Fetch `MissionKnowledgePayload`.
5. Fetch `GoalsKnowledgePayload`.
6. Inject them into the corresponding section builders (e.g., BAB I and BAB II).
The Assembler composes; the Builder projects.

## 11. Future Registry Audit
**Future Goals Knowledge Registry Design**:
- **Registry**: Exposes a standardized endpoint, e.g., `registry.getGoals(schoolId, academicYear)`.
- **Knowledge Object**: Returns a strongly-typed `CanonicalKnowledgeObject<GoalsKnowledgeDomain>`.
- **Assembler**: Awaits the specific registry call.
- **Builder**: Remains completely oblivious to the registry, consuming the interface to map to Canonical Blocks.

## 12. Technical Debt Audit
The current architecture tightly couples Goals into `VisionMissionPayload`. This prevents proper Goals separation (violating Rule 086 and Rule 070) because the Goals lifecycle (annual) is forced into the same payload as the Vision lifecycle (4-8 years). This impurity is documented under **TD-KSP-002**.
