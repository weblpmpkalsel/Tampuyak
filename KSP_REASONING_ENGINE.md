# KSP REASONING ENGINE

## Concept
Narrative generation must be preceded by a logical reasoning process. The Reasoning Engine prevents the creation of hollow, template-based paragraphs.

## Mechanism
Before writing the KSP, the `KSPIntelligenceEngine` executes:
1. **Data Ingestion**: Gathers Rapor Pendidikan, School Profile, and Regional Potential.
2. **Matrix Generation**: Builds the School Characteristic Matrix (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Challenges).
3. **Alignment Verification**: Ensures that the drafted Vision aligns with the Matrix, and the Mission directly addresses the Weaknesses and Challenges.

## Example Flow
`Education Report (Low Numeracy) -> Reasoning Tree (Identify need for numeric integration) -> KSP Component 4 (Integration of Numeracy across subjects)`

## Result
Every generated component represents a strategic response to actual school conditions.
