# KSP GOLD M3.0A: BAB III Knowledge Classification Audit

## Executive Summary
This document provides the Knowledge Classification Audit for BAB III (Pengorganisasian Pembelajaran) prior to its implementation. By strictly identifying the ownership, lifecycle, and source of truth for every subsection, we ensure that the KSP GOLD engine remains a pure orchestrator of enterprise knowledge, avoiding the generation of volatile or hallucinatory administrative content.

## Detailed Analysis

### Document Narrative vs. Enterprise Knowledge
Most of BAB III is purely **Enterprise Knowledge**. Traditional KSP generation tools treat this chapter as a giant document narrative, leading to copy-pasting and manual hallucination. In KSP GOLD, BAB III is an assembly of Operational, Strategic, and Teacher Knowledge domains. There is almost zero pure "document narrative" other than transition sentences bridging the tables and lists.

### Volatile vs. Stable Knowledge
- **Stable Knowledge (Embeddable)**: Learning Load, Curriculum Structure, Extracurricular Lists, P5 Themes. These change yearly and are safe to embed directly into the Canonical Document as `TABLE` or `LIST` blocks.
- **Volatile Knowledge (Attachments Only)**: Teaching Modules (Modul Ajar), ATP, and detailed Intracurricular syllabus data. These change weekly/monthly and belong to the **Teacher Knowledge** domain. Embedding them would bloat the KSP document and break the single source of truth. They MUST be linked via the Attachment Gateway.

## GO / NO GO
**GO**.
The classification strictly separates static regulatory frameworks and school operational data from volatile teacher artifacts (Modul Ajar, ATP). This ensures that the KSP generation engine remains a stable architectural orchestrator. BAB III implementation can proceed safely without violating enterprise boundaries.
