# GDC-6.0 BAB I EDITORIAL CERTIFICATION

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This certification addresses the editorial quality of BAB I (Karakteristik Satuan Pendidikan) in the generated KSP document. Prior to this audit, BAB I relied heavily on simplistic fallback narratives or directly injected raw payloads without context. The goal was to transform BAB I into an editorial-quality Government document that reads professionally, adheres to the Golden Narrative Pattern, and aligns seamlessly with Kemendikdasmen standards.

The document structure for BAB I has been completely refactored to employ a multi-layered narrative approach (Opening -> Context/Data -> Closing) across all subsections, completely eliminating filler, "Data belum tersedia," and application-level wording.
