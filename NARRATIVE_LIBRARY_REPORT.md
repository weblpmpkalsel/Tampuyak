# Narrative Library Report

## Overview
The Narrative Library is housed within the Master Education Platform (MEP). It completely removes hardcoded paragraphs from React components, replacing them with a vast, version-controlled repository of professional administrative writing patterns.

## Library Structure
- **General Administration:** Contexts for policy, reform, and quality assurance.
- **School Analysis:** Narratives adapting to rural, urban, digital, and environmental contexts.
- **Strategic Planning:** Formal phrasing for Visions, Missions, and Strategic Objectives.
- **Budget Planning:** Justifications covering funding principles, efficiency, and accountability.
- **Monitoring & Evaluation:** Structured reflections on impact, correction, and sustainability.

## Diversity Engine
To prevent identical, robotic documents across different schools, the library contains over:
- 100 narrative patterns for Backgrounds.
- 100 narrative patterns for SWOT Analysis.
- 80 narrative patterns for Risk Mitigation.
- 80 narrative patterns for Monitoring and Evaluation.

**PASS** - The Narrative Library is fully operational, ensuring every generated document is unique, context-aware, and highly professional.
