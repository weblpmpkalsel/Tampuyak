# RKAS REBUILD REPORT

## Architecture Overhaul
- **Paradigm Shift:** The RKAS module was completely decoupled from the Government Writing Engine (GWE). It is no longer a narrative-dominant essay.
- **Financial Matrix Dominance:** Re-architected as a structured school budget planning document. The majority of the output now consists of Financial Planning Tables and Budget Recapitulations.
- **Narrative Purge:** Removed repetitive introductions, conclusions, educational philosophy paragraphs, and generic AI writing.

## Structure Rebuilt
The RKAS document now rigorously follows official budgeting layouts:
- **Chapter I:** Concise Introduction
- **Chapter II:** Planning Summary
- **Chapter III:** Priority Programs
- **Chapter IV:** Budget Plan (Tabular Financial Matrix)
- **Chapter V:** Closing

## Outcome
The RKAS now accurately serves its intended function: an official school financial planning document, devoid of unnecessary wordiness and optimized for auditing.
