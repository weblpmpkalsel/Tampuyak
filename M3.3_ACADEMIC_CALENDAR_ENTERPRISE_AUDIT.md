# KSP GOLD M3.3: Academic Calendar Enterprise Shared Domain Audit

## Executive Summary
This document executes the Enterprise Shared Domain Audit for the Academic Calendar. The audit establishes the Academic Calendar as a centralized, shared Enterprise Knowledge Domain. It proves ownership, lifecycle, and source of truth to ensure the calendar is fully reusable across TAMPUYAK OS (KSP, Teacher Workspace, Planning, etc.) without duplication.

## Section D: Lifecycle Matrix
- **Static / Multi-Year:** None (Calendar shifts yearly).
- **Academic Year (Annual):** Master Calendar baseline (Holidays, Semester boundaries, Total Effective Weeks, MPLS, Asesmen Nasional).
- **Semester:** Assessment periods, Report distribution, P5 block scheduling.
- **Monthly/Weekly:** Specific school events, incidental holidays (Libur Khusus).

## Section E: Enterprise Consumers
The Academic Calendar is a core shared domain consumed by:
1. **KSP:** Renders the static yearly overview for compliance (BAB III).
2. **Teacher Workspace:** Dictates pacing for Modul Ajar, ATP milestones, and instructional days.
3. **Planning (RKT/RKAS):** Aligns budgeting and resource allocation with school events.
4. **Attendance & Scheduling:** Differentiates active school days from holidays.
5. **Dashboard & AI:** Provides temporal context for notifications and AI insights.

## Section F: Duplication Analysis
**Finding:** Currently, there is a high risk of KSP defining its own calendar events locally, which would fall out of sync with the Teacher Workspace and Attendance modules.
**Rule:** KSP SHALL NOT duplicate calendar data. It MUST consume the master calendar payload from a shared Enterprise source. Manual entry of calendar dates directly into KSP is strictly forbidden.

## Section H: Registry Readiness
**Decision: YES, an Academic Calendar Registry is required.**
- **Responsibilities:** Merge Government holidays, Regional education office dates, and School-specific events into a single, unified chronological timeline. Calculate effective weeks dynamically.
- **Expected Payload:** An immutable array of `CalendarEvent` objects and a summarized `AcademicTime` object.
- **Builder Independence:** KSP Builders will simply accept this pre-calculated array and project it into Canonical Blocks.

## Section I: Canonical Mapping
All required structures can be represented using existing blocks:
- **`TABLE`**: To display month-by-month event schedules or semester summaries.
- **`LIST`**: To summarize key dates (e.g., list of national holidays).
- **`PARAGRAPH`**: For calendar policies and explanatory text.
- **`ATTACHMENT`**: (Optional) For attaching the highly detailed graphical calendar poster if the school prefers not to use tables.
*Conclusion: No new Canonical Blocks are required.*

## Section J: Implementation Strategy & Assembler/Builder Roles

### Assembler Strategy
`KspDocumentAssembler` will fetch the shared Academic Calendar payload from the `EnterpriseMasterDataRegistry` (or its adapter) and pass it immutably into `LearningOrganizationBuilder` or a dedicated `AcademicCalendarBuilder`.

### Builder Strategy
The Builder remains a **Pure Consumer**. It iterates over the timeline and generates a `TABLE` block. It performs NO date math, NO holiday lookups, and NO effective week calculations.

### Files Expected to Change
- `src/enterprise/master-data/AcademicYear.model.ts` (To define the shared `CalendarEvent` interface)
- `src/enterprise/knowledge/services/LearningOrganizationService.ts` (To process and validate the new calendar events)
- `src/enterprise/document/ksp/bab3/KspBab3Structure.ts` (To render the calendar table)
- `src/ksp/KspMetadataAdapter.ts` (To supply mock/workspace events)

## Risk Assessment
- **Risk:** Calendar complexity (e.g., overlapping events, shifting national holidays).
- **Mitigation:** Push all complexity to the future Academic Calendar Registry. KSP Builder only renders what it is given.
