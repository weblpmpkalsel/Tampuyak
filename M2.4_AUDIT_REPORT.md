## 3. Mission Knowledge Audit
- **Purpose**: Defines the strategic actions and operational commitments the school undertakes to achieve its aspirational Vision. It bridges the gap between current reality and the future state.
- **Lifecycle**: Medium-to-long term. A Mission is typically reviewed every 1-3 years and adjusted based on shifting school conditions or unfulfilled goals, even if the Vision remains the same.
- **Ownership**: The School's Governing Body, Principal, and educational team.
- **Consumers**: KSP Document Builders (BAB II), RKT/RKAS Planning Engines, and Accreditation Authorities.

## 4. Knowledge Ownership Audit
- **School**: The true authoritative owner. The school leadership and stakeholders formulate these actionable steps.
- **Government**: Merely provides regulatory guidelines but does not dictate the specific strategic mission of individual schools.
- **Workspace**: Acts only as a temporary conduit.
- **Future Registry**: Will act as the enterprise system of record and technical owner of the digital asset.

## 5. Knowledge Source Audit
- **Origin**: Mission Knowledge originates from formal school policy documents or deliberation processes (e.g., school committee meetings).
- **Current Flow**: Entered manually by users and passed through Workspace Metadata into the `VisionMissionPayload`.
- **Future Flow**: Will be queried deterministically from the Future Registry. The system must never use AI to generate or hallucinate a school's mission.

## 6. Knowledge Independence Audit
- **From Vision**: A Vision is the long-term destination. The Mission contains the strategic steps to reach it. The Mission can pivot or adapt while the Vision remains constant.
- **From Goals**: Goals are measurable, short-term targets derived from the Mission. Goals change annually; Mission statements are more stable.
- **From School Knowledge**: Changes in demographics or infrastructure inform the Mission but are structurally separate.
- **From Regulatory Knowledge**: Entirely separate. Government mandates are external constraints; the Mission is internal strategy.

## 7. Relationship Audit
**Dependency Chain**:
`School Knowledge` (Current state and constraints) 
  ↓ influences
`Vision Knowledge` (Where the school wants to be)
  ↓ drives
`Mission Knowledge` (What the school will do to get there)
  ↓ measures
`Goals Knowledge` (Specific, measurable targets for the actions)
  ↓
`Document Projection` (The Document Builder simply projects these independent domains into a readable format like BAB II).

## 8. Builder Audit
Builders must remain pure Consumers. A Builder will take the `MissionKnowledgePayload` and map it strictly to a `LIST` or `PARAGRAPH` Canonical Block. 
Builders shall never:
- Rewrite, summarize, or optimize the mission using AI.
- Generate a mission if one is missing.

## 9. Assembler Audit
The `KspDocumentAssembler` operates as the orchestrator:
1. Fetch `RegulatoryKnowledgePayload`.
2. Fetch `SchoolKnowledgePayload`.
3. Fetch `VisionKnowledgePayload`.
4. Fetch `MissionKnowledgePayload`.
5. Inject them into the appropriate section builders (e.g., BAB I and BAB II).
The Assembler composes; the Builder projects.

## 10. Registry Audit
**Future Mission Knowledge Registry Design**:
- **Registry**: Exposes a standardized endpoint, e.g., `registry.getMission(schoolId)`.
- **Knowledge Object**: Returns a strongly-typed `CanonicalKnowledgeObject<MissionKnowledgeDomain>`.
- **Assembler**: Awaits the specific registry calls and passes the payloads downstream.
- **Builder**: Remains completely oblivious to the registry, consuming the interface to map to Canonical Blocks.

## 11. Technical Debt Audit
The existing `VisionMissionPayload` violates Rule 086 ("Vision Knowledge ≠ Mission Knowledge") and Rule 070 ("Knowledge Domains are independent"). It is a composite payload acting as a transitional artifact (Rule 087). 
This impurity is documented under **TD-KSP-002**. Because architecturally superior solutions shall not violate Zero Regression (Rule 088) and sprint objectives override roadmap ambitions (Rule 089), this technical debt is accepted (Rule 090).
