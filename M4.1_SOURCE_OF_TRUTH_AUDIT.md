## 2. SOURCE MATRIX
| Knowledge Item | Owner | Source of Truth | Consumer |

| Pendampingan (Mentoring) | Kepala Sekolah / Pengawas | `PlanningIntelligenceEngine` (RKT) | `Bab4Builder` |

| Evaluasi (Rapor Pendidikan) | Kepala Sekolah | `RaporIntelligenceEngine` | `Bab4Builder` |

| Pengembangan Profesional | Tim Pengembang Kurikulum | `MasterData` (Guru/Tendik) / `PlanningIntelligenceEngine` | `Bab4Builder` |

## 3. OWNERSHIP MATRIX
* **Kepala Sekolah (Principal):** Owns the overarching Evaluation and Monitoring framework.
* **Tim Pengembang Kurikulum (TPK):** Owns the operational implementation of curriculum development and mentoring schedules.
* **Tim Penjaminan Mutu (Quality Assurance):** Owns internal evaluation forms and monitoring timelines.
* **Pengawas Sekolah (Supervisor):** Owns external validation and serves as a stakeholder for evaluation results.

## 4. LIFECYCLE MATRIX
* **Pendampingan & Pengembangan Profesional:** Annual lifecycle. Generated during the beginning of the academic year based on RKT.
* **Monitoring & Evaluasi:** Semester/Annual lifecycle. Evaluated at mid-term or end-of-year.
* **Tindak Lanjut:** Continuous lifecycle, feeding directly back into next year's RKT and RKJM.

## 5. ATTACHMENT STRATEGY
To keep the canonical document strictly focused and readable, the following detailed operational items will be treated as **Attachments** rather than embedded directly into BAB IV:
* Instrumen Monitoring / Supervisi Akademik (Supervision Instruments)
* Rekapitulasi Rapor Pendidikan Lengkap (Full Education Report)
* Jadwal Detail Pelatihan/Workshop (Detailed Training Schedules)
* Notulen Rapat Evaluasi (Meeting Minutes)
The main BAB IV text will provide the framework, summary, and reference the attachments explicitly.

## 6. BUILDER STRATEGY
Builders (e.g., `KspBab4Builder`) will remain **Pure Consumers**.
* **No Business Rules:** The builder will not determine *how* evaluation is done; it will only format what `EvaluationService` provides.
* **No Calculations:** Scoring or metric aggregations belong to the respective intelligence engines.
* **No Planning Logic:** Mentoring programs will be fetched directly from `PlanningIntelligenceEngine`.

## 7. ASSEMBLER STRATEGY
The `KspDocumentAssembler` will compose the BAB IV payload by invoking the respective knowledge builders and orchestrating the final document structure. It will strictly avoid generating duplicated management knowledge and will rely solely on authoritative sources.

## 8. CANONICAL MAPPING
The existing Canonical Document schema is fully sufficient to express all of BAB IV:
* **HEADING:** Sections and Subsections.
* **PARAGRAPH:** Framework narratives and policies.
* **LIST:** Bulleted strategies and goals.
* **TABLE:** Summary schedules, evaluation matrices, and professional development plans.
* **ATTACHMENT:** References to external instruments and detailed reports.

## 9. REGISTRY READINESS
The architecture naturally aligns with future registry implementations:
* **Evaluation Registry:** Will seamlessly replace or enhance `EvaluationService`.
* **Professional Development Registry:** Will integrate with `MasterData` (Guru/Tendik) to track individual capacity building.
* **Planning Registry:** Already partially established via `PlanningIntelligenceEngine`.

## 10. RISK ASSESSMENT
* **Architecture Risk:** LOW. The separation of concerns is explicitly maintained.
* **Data Duplication Risk:** ZERO. Strict adherence to single Source of Truth prevents conflicting data.
* **Implementation Risk:** LOW. No new Canonical Blocks needed. The primary requirement is robust querying from existing engines.
