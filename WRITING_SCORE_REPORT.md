# Writing Score Report

## Scoring Algorithm
The DRE calculates a quantitative Writing Score based on:
- **Professionalism (30%):** Absence of informal tone and marketing fluff.
- **Richness (30%):** Sufficient paragraph length and multi-layered composition (Background -> Problem -> Strategy).
- **Consistency (20%):** Absolute adherence to the Terminology Dictionary.
- **Structure (20%):** Proper use of introductions and conclusions around tables and lists.

## Thresholds
- **95 - 100:** Enterprise Ready
- **90 - 94:** Very Good
- **80 - 89:** Needs Minor Revision
- **< 80:** Revision Required

Documents scoring below 95 receive specific, actionable correction suggestions and cannot proceed to the final certification gate.

## Final Certification
**PASS** - Writing scores are accurately calculated and utilized as a strict quality gate.
