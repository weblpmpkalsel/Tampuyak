# PLANNING & QUALITY RELATIONSHIP
## TAMPUYAK OS ENTERPRISE ARCHITECTURE

### 1. THE BOUNDARY PRINCIPLE
The architecture of Tampuyak OS enforces a strict decoupling between Planning (RKJM, RKT, RKAS) and Evaluation (Supervision, Analytics). 
**CRITICAL RULE: The Quality Engine (Academic Supervision) MUST NEVER automatically mutate the Planning Engine.**

Planning remains the indisputable Single Source of Truth for school strategy. The Quality Engine functions as an observational overlay that produces Recommendations, not mutations.

### 2. DATA PROVENANCE & FLOW

#### A. Read-Only Projections (Planning -> Quality)
The Quality Engine consumes data from the Planning Engine strictly as immutable references:
- **Academic Calendar:** Dictates the allowable scheduling windows for Supervision.
- **RKT Objectives:** Sets the contextual focus for Observation Instruments (e.g., if RKT prioritizes Digital Literacy, the Supervision Instrument projects this focus).
- **KSP Paradigms:** Determines the baseline pedagogical standards the Quality Engine must measure against.

#### B. Evidentiary Data (Internal to Quality)
Data generated during execution and measurement belongs exclusively to the Quality Engine:
- **Observation Records:** Assessor scoring and notes.
- **Evidence:** Attached modules, photos, and reflections.
- **RTL Progress:** Tracking of follow-up action plans.
*Note: None of this data flows directly into the RKT or RKAS.*

#### C. Recommendations (Quality -> Planning)
The output of the Quality Engine is synthesized into **Quality Recommendations**.
- **Nature of Recommendations:** These are structured proposals (e.g., "70% of teachers scored low in differentiated instruction. Recommend allocating RKAS budget for Differentiated Instruction Workshop next semester.").
- **Handling Mechanism:** Recommendations are placed into a "Review Queue" for School Executives during the next Planning Review cycle.

#### D. Planning Reference (Human-in-the-Loop Mutation)
- Executives review the Recommendations generated by Quality Analytics.
- If approved, a human Executive manually authors a new RKJM, RKT, or RKAS entry based on that recommendation.
- **Conclusion:** The mutation of the Planning Engine is always a deliberate administrative action informed by the Quality Engine, never a programmatic side-effect.
