## Conclusion
**OVERALL STATUS: PASS**

The Domain Layer is successfully decoupled from the Presentation Layer. 
1. **Content Parity:** 100% of paragraphs and tables are preserved across all 4 enterprise documents.
2. **Layout Integrity:** Page counts are stable; the single-page reduction in RKT is an objective improvement due to cleaner landscape CSS bridging.
3. **No Regressions:** PDF and DOCX file sizes remain within expected bounds, proving no content truncation or bloat occurred during generation.

The Golden Master validation confirms the system is stable and the architecture refactor is a complete success.
