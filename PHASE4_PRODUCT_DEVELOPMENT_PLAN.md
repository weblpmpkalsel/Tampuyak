# PHASE 4 PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PLAN
**Checkpoint:** OMEGA-PHASE4-PRODUCT-DEVELOPMENT
**Project:** TAMPUYAK OS
**Date:** 2026-07-13

## 1. Business Modules (Priority List)

### Priority A (Core Operations)
- RKAS Workflow & Integrasi
- RKAS Approval & Digital Signature
- Export Manager (PDF, Excel, Print)
- Versioning & Audit Trail Dasar

### Priority B (Monitoring & Insight)
- Executive Dashboard
- Monitoring Capaian (RKJM, RKT, RKAS)
- Analytics Dasar & Pelaporan

### Priority C (Compliance & Extended Documents)
- BOS (Bantuan Operasional Sekolah)
- Adiwiyata (Program Sekolah Peduli Lingkungan)
- EDS (Evaluasi Diri Sekolah)

### Priority D (Intelligence & Advanced Features)
- AI Recommendation Engine
- Smart Validation
- Audit Intelligence & Anomaly Detection

## 2. Release Roadmap (Tampuyak OS Enterprise Release 1.0)

- **Milestone 1: Core Document Migration & Viewing (Completed)**
  - Canonical Platform v2.1 FROZEN.
  - RKJM, RKT, RKAS terintegrasi via Adapter dan bisa dirender sempurna.
- **Milestone 2: Interaction & Export (Q3 2026)**
  - Implementasi Pagination Engine V2.
  - PDF & Print Export Manager.
  - Persetujuan (Approval) Workflow Dasar.
- **Milestone 3: Data & Intelligence (Q4 2026)**
  - Dashboard Monitoring.
  - Implementasi AI Recommendation untuk pembuatan dokumen (Knowledge Graph, dsb).
- **Milestone 4: Tampuyak OS Enterprise Release 1.0 (Q1 2027)**
  - UAT Final (User Acceptance Testing).
  - Launching Enterprise Edition (Production).

## FINAL DECLARATION
Mulai Phase 4, seluruh pengembangan Tampuyak OS berfokus pada Business Layer dan nilai fungsional bagi pengguna. Canonical Platform, Migration Framework, dan Enterprise Publishing Foundation telah dianggap stabil dan dibekukan sebagai fondasi produksi. Perubahan terhadap fondasi hanya diperbolehkan melalui Architecture Decision Record (ADR) yang disetujui.
